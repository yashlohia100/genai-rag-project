# Zephyr Labs Employee Handbook (Sample)

## Remote work
Zephyr Labs is remote-first. Employees may work from any country where Zephyr has a legal entity: India, Portugal, and Canada. Working from another country for more than 30 days in a year requires approval from the People team.

Every employee receives a one-time home office stipend of 60,000 INR (or the local equivalent), plus 2,000 INR per month for internet.

## Core hours
Core collaboration hours are 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM IST, Monday to Thursday. Fridays are "focus Fridays": no internal meetings are scheduled.

## Leave
Employees get 24 days of paid leave per year, plus public holidays of their country. Unused leave carries over up to a maximum of 10 days. Parental leave is 26 weeks fully paid for all parents.

The company shuts down entirely for the last week of December.

## Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 50,000 INR for courses, books, and conferences. Requests above 20,000 INR need manager approval. The budget does not roll over.

## Equipment
New joiners choose between a MacBook Pro 14" and a ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to it-help@zephyr.example within 48 hours.
